## EN
A glossy wood with an attractive golden color that adds a warm artistic touch to any space.

**Characteristics**
- Bright golden-yellow color
- Hard and durable with straight grain
- Used as an economical alternative to premium yellow hardwoods

**Uses**
- Luxurious flooring with a warm character
- Elegant furniture with modern designs
- Distinctive veneer for doors and décor
- Fine carpentry and high-end decorative work
