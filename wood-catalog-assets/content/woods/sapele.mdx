## EN
A luxurious-looking wood with a rich reddish color and distinctive stripes that give it a strong visual presence.

**Characteristics**
- Reddish-brown color that darkens over time
- Interlocked grain that creates a distinctive striped appearance
- Durable and resistant to decay

**Uses**
- High-end furniture with an elegant classic look
- Premium interior and exterior doors
- Luxurious flooring suitable for heavy use
- Professional musical instruments
- High-quality veneer for upscale projects
