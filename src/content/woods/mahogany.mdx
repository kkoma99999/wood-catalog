## EN
A globally renowned luxury wood that combines beauty with ease of machining, making it one of the best choices for high-end applications.

**Characteristics**
- Red-brown color with straight grain
- Durable and easy to work with

**Uses**
- High-end furniture with premium finishes
- Luxurious interior décor
- Boat and yacht manufacturing
- Doors and veneer for distinguished projects

**الخصائص**
