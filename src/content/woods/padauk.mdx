## EN
A distinctive red-colored wood that catches the eye, with high strength and durability.

**Characteristics**
- Coral-red color that turns into reddish-brown
- Very stable and easy to work with
- Resistant to decay

**Uses**
- Luxury furniture with bold designs
- Artistic work and carving
- High-quality tool handles
- Premium flooring
- Distinctive veneer for upscale projects
