## EN
A lightweight wood with a smooth texture, known for its ease of shaping and natural sheen, making it one of the most in-demand woods in modern industries.

**Characteristics**
- Shiny surface with straight to slightly interlocked grain
- Dries quickly and produces good-quality timber

**Uses**
- High-quality plywood and blockboard manufacturing
- Modern interior décor
- Lightweight furniture with contemporary designs
- Premium veneer for doors and furniture
