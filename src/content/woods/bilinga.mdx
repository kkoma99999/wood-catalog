## EN
A very strong wood that is highly resistant to external conditions, making it ideal for heavy-duty applications.

**Characteristics**
- Yellow-orange color
- Very hard and resistant to weather conditions

**Uses**
- Wooden bridges and external structural works
- Outdoor flooring resistant to water
- Garden walkways and decking
- Construction applications requiring high strength
