## EN
A dark, luxurious wood with a deep chocolate-brown color that gives any space an elegant and sophisticated appearance.

**Characteristics**
- Dark brown color with black streaks
- Very heavy and extremely hard
- Naturally resistant to termites

**Uses**
- Luxury furniture with contemporary designs
- High-durability flooring
- Premium interior décor
- Professional musical instruments
- Dark veneer for upscale projects
