## EN
A wood rich in natural oils, resistant to water and weather conditions, and considered one of the strongest woods in the world.

**Characteristics**
- Rich in natural oils
- Resistant to water and weather conditions
- Very stable

**Uses**
- Luxury outdoor furniture that lasts for many years
- Outdoor decking resistant to weather conditions
- Boat and yacht manufacturing
- Outdoor kitchens and marine applications
