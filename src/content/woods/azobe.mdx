## EN
One of the strongest woods in the world, resistant to water, salinity, and insects.

**Characteristics**
- One of the heaviest woods in the world
- Resistant to water, insects, and weather conditions

**Uses**
- Marine piers and ports
- Bridges and heavy structures
- Railway sleepers
- Applications requiring exceptional strength and long-term durability
